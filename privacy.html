Privacy Policy — Sales OS (personal)

Last updated: 12 November 2026

What this app is

Sales OS is a personal, self-hosted tool operated by Mohamed Yasser on his own computer. It connects only to Mohamed's own Gmail account. It is not a public service and no other person signs in to it.

Google user data accessed

  • gmail.send — to send business emails that Mohamed has authorized, from his own account.
  • gmail.readonly — to read replies and delivery notices in Mohamed's own inbox, so the tool can record a reply, stop follow-up emails, and respect opt-out requests.

How the data is used

  • Data is used only to send Mohamed's own outreach emails and to process replies to them.
  • Data is stored only on Mohamed's own computer. It is not uploaded to any third-party server.
  • Data is never sold, shared, transferred, or used for advertising, and is never used to train AI models.
  • Recipients can opt out at any time by replying; opt-out requests are honored automatically and permanently.

Compliance

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections.
